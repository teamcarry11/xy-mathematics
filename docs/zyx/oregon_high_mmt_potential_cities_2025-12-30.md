## MMT Framework: Why Oregon Cities Are Optimal

### Tax Structure (MMT-Aligned)

**Oregon's Advantages**:
- **No sales tax**: Eliminates most regressive form of taxation
- **Progressive income tax**: 4.75% to 9.9% (targets high earners, not working-class)
- **Lower property tax**: 0.81% average (reasonable)
- **Result**: Working-class people keep most of their income, maximizing effective demand

**Comparison to Other States**:
- Better than no-income-tax states with high sales taxes (Nevada 6.85%, Texas 8.25%, Washington 9.43-10.4%)
- Better than high-tax states with regressive sales taxes (California 7.5% sales tax)
- Optimal MMT structure: Eliminates regressive taxation while targeting high earners for inflation management

**Key Sectors**:
- Technology/Software (Grain OS potential)
- Manufacturing (150,000+ jobs in Portland metro)
- Healthcare (growing sector)
- Construction (strong demand)
- Education (community colleges, vocational training)

**Vocational Training Access**:
- Community colleges (statewide network)

- Apprenticeship programs (Hillsboro, others)
- Alternative education paths (vocational, technical)

## Grain OS Entry-Level Job Paths

### Recommended Training Sequences

**Path 1: Community College → Associate Degree → Entry-Level Job**
1. Enroll in community college technology program
2. Complete associate degree (2 years)
3. Build portfolio during studies
4. Apply for entry-level tech jobs
5. **Grain OS Entry Point**: Entry-level developer, IT support, QA tester

**Path 2: Coding Bootcamp → Portfolio → Entry-Level Job**
1. Enroll in coding bootcamp (Lane CC, Portland options, online)
2. Complete bootcamp (18 weeks to 6 months)
3. Build portfolio with projects
4. Apply for entry-level developer positions
5. **Grain OS Entry Point**: Entry-level developer, junior software engineer

**Path 3: Apprenticeship → Certification → Tech Job**
1. Enroll in technical apprenticeship (Hillsboro, others)
2. Complete paid training (1-2 years)
3. Earn certification (BOLI, industry certifications)
4. Apply for tech jobs
5. **Grain OS Entry Point**: Technical support, system administration, entry-level developer

**Path 4: Self-Study + Portfolio → Entry-Level Job**
1. Complete online courses (free/paid)
2. Build portfolio with projects
3. Contribute to open source (Grain OS potential)
4. Apply for entry-level positions
5. **Grain OS Entry Point**: Entry-level developer, contributor, intern
