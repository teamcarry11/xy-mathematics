## Executive Summary

This document outlines a strategic approach to securing federal grants and congressional support for Grain OS projects that benefit both Oregon counties and Sonoma County, California. The strategy focuses on:

1. **Federal Grant Opportunities**: NSF programs (POSE, GEO OSE, CSSI), Department of Labor workforce development grants
2. **Congressional Outreach**: Bipartisan technology innovation champions (Ro Khanna D-CA, Mike Gallagher R-WI, others)
3. **Plain English Communication**: Framing Grain OS in terms of workforce development, ecological sustainability, and convivial technology
4. **Cross-State Collaboration**: Oregon-California workforce development partnerships
5. **Local Leverage**: Bay Area and Sacramento metropolitan areas for networking and support

**Key Framing**: Present Grain OS as:
- **Workforce Development**: Vocational training, entry-level tech jobs, economic opportunity
- **Ecological Sustainability**: Open-source technology, appropriate technology, convivial tools
- **Innovation Leadership**: U.S. technology competitiveness, open-source ecosystems
- **Cross-State Collaboration**: Oregon-California partnerships, regional economic development
